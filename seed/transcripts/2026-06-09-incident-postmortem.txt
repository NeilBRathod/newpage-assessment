[00:01:15] Dana Osei: Because the index it was using stopped being used. We deployed a change on the fourth that added a filter on settlement status to that query. The planner switched to a sequential scan. On a table that was small in staging and is not small in production.
[00:01:34] Jamie Fox: And staging didn't catch it because staging has about four thousand rows and production has eleven million.
[00:01:42] Dana Osei: Correct.
[00:01:44] Priya Raman: So we have three separate failures and I want to name all three.
One, a query regression we didn't catch. Two, a connection pool with no headroom and no
alerting. Three, ninety-five minutes of blindness. Any one of those alone is survivable.
Together they're an outage.
